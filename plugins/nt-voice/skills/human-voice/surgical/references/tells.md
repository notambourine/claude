# Tell reference

Flag, never ban: delve, tapestry, landscape, realm, underscore, highlight, leverage,
seamless, robust, crucial, pivotal, vital, testament, navigate, foster, cultivate, elevate,
unlock, empower, myriad, plethora, game-changer, revolutionary.

Prefer: look, name the thing, show, use, reliable, explain consequence, handle, build,
improve, many, concrete change.

Cut: "in today's world", "when it comes to", "worth noting", "at the end of the day".
Replace "that being said" with "but" when needed. Name specific tradeoffs instead of
cliches.

Structural flags: uniform paragraphs/sentences, definition-openings, needless lists, false
balance, numbered signposting, bold saturation, dash saturation, symmetrical hedging, tidy
conclusions.

Validate: meaning lost? Formula or choice? Announcement or content? Heat or information?
More trustworthy without it?

Personal voice: light touch. Marketing: heavier scrutiny. Preserve register.
