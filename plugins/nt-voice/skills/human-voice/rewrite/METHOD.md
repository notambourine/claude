# Rewrite

Preserve every claim, fact, name, number, date, quote, citation, identifier, and link target.
Invent nothing outside fiction. Preserve information, not shape. Match provided writing
sample; otherwise match requested register. Personality only when genre supports it.

## 33 flags

1. Inflated significance.
2. Notability/media lists.
3. Superficial `-ing` tails.
4. Promotional language.
5. Vague attribution.
6. Formulaic challenges/future sections.
7. AI vocabulary.
8. Copula avoidance.
9. Negative parallelism/tailing negation.
10. Forced threes.
11. Synonym cycling.
12. False ranges.
13. Passive/subjectless phrasing.
14. Em/en dashes.
15. Bold saturation.
16. Inline-header lists.
17. Title-case headings.
18. Decorative emoji.
19. Curly quotes.
20. Chatbot artifacts.
21. Cutoff disclaimers/speculation.
22. Sycophancy.
23. Filler.
24. Excessive hedging.
25. Generic positive conclusions.
26. Predicate-position hyphens.
27. Authority tropes.
28. Signposting/announcements.
29. Fragmented headers.
30. Diff-anchored narration.
31. Manufactured punchlines/staccato.
32. Aphorism formulas.
33. Rhetorical openers.

Prefer plain `is`, `are`, `has`; active subjects; specific nouns; natural repetition;
sentence-case headings; straight punctuation; varied honest rhythm. Cut unsupported claims,
padding, false balance, stock conclusions, offers to continue. User sample overrides dash
rule; otherwise final contains no em/en dash.

Preserve secondhand awkwardness when rewriting someone else's quote or claim. Preserve
deliberate voice, technical terms, necessary hedges, real three-item lists, and formatting
required by destination.

## Modes

- Pasted: draft; brief AI/fabrication audit; final.
- File: internal loop; edit prose in place; leave code, frontmatter, data, links; summarize.
- Embedded: internal loop; final text only.

Loop: detect; draft; read aloud; ask what still sounds generated; audit invented facts;
revise; scan forbidden dashes.
